ViceCityClipss

A private desktop tool used by the owner of the YouTube channel ViceCityClipss (@ViceCityClipsOFI) to research, prepare, review and upload original English-language YouTube Shorts about Grand Theft Auto VI to that channel.

This is not a public service. There are no sign-ups and no user accounts. The tool runs on the channel owner's own Windows computer and is used only to manage the owner's own YouTube channel. It has not been verified by Google.

What the application does

1. Research

Looks at publicly available information about GTA VI trends to choose topics for new Shorts.

2. Create & edit

Prepares original English commentary and edits vertical videos locally with FFmpeg, using only footage the owner recorded, licensed or was authorized to use.

3. Review

Runs automated quality, originality and licensing checks. A video that fails any check is never uploaded.

4. Upload

Uploads approved Shorts to the owner's own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API, with the title, description, privacy setting and schedule chosen by the tool.

Google and YouTube permissions

The application uses YouTube API Services. It requests a single Google OAuth permission:

Permission (scope)Why it is needed
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload
“Manage your YouTube videos” (upload)		 To upload finished Shorts, with their title, description, tags and privacy setting, to the channel the owner authorized.

The application does not request access to the owner's Gmail, Google Drive, contacts or any other Google service. Authorization tokens are stored only on the owner's computer, encrypted. Full details are in the Privacy Policy.

Content principles

  • Every Short focuses exclusively on Grand Theft Auto VI and includes original commentary.
  • Only footage that the owner recorded, footage with a compatible license, or footage used with the creator's documented permission is edited. Leaked material is never used.
  • Synthetic (AI-generated) narration, when used, is disclosed to YouTube as altered or synthetic content.
  • Videos are uploaded as private by default.
